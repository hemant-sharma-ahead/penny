Track your expenses card: one card instead of a bare "Spent ₹0" column, now with two CTAs — + Add account (straight to Accounts, for someone who wants an account set up first) and Go to Expenses (for bank-statement import or bringing expenses in from another app). Between the two, this also closes the reachability gap: Accounts gets a direct route again, and Expenses is where the import flows already live either way.

Track Insurance / Track Loans: two small, honest prompt cards replacing those columns' dashes — same visual language as the expenses card, each routing straight to its own screen. This is also what actually solves the reachability problem for these two: they keep a real, visible entry point on Home instead of a silent "—" that a new user has no reason to tap.

Financial Health — copy fix: kept your layout (you said the card itself feels good), replaced the vague "Your score shows up once there's something to score" with copy that says outright what the score is — a 0–100 number across 6 concrete areas — before asking for engagement. The ring shows "0–100" as a neutral range indicator instead of a specific grade, so there's nothing looking like a real (and possibly alarming) result yet.

Also fixed in code (not a mockup change): the "On track — fully funded" chip below the net-worth chart was showing on a completely empty app — root-caused to the retirement target (corpusNeeded) degenerating to 0 with no expense data, which a simple gap > 0 check misread as "already funded." Now shows "Add your monthly expenses to calculate your retirement target" instead when there's no real target yet. Also fixed the "today" corpus label's light-theme readability for good — the actual cause was a hardcoded black corner-glow blob (designed for dark theme's own dark background) sitting directly under that label, muddying it in light theme regardless of the label's own text color.
