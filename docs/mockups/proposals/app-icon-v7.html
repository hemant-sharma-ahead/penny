Fresh — ten coin-first directions
1. The Rising Stack

Three coins stacked, each one bigger than the one below — a pile that widens as it climbs, instead of the usual pile that tapers. Unmistakably coins, unmistakably growing.

2. Growth Line Coin

A simple rising investment line struck into the coin's face, ending in a marker dot. Direct, finance-literate, no interpretation required.

3. Ascending Ribbon

A coin with a flowing ribbon-arrow rising out of it and off the edge — motion and direction, not a flat static arrow.

4. Penny Into Gold

A small coin, a dotted trail, a big coin — the same coin, grown, told through scale alone. "Chip in" small, watch it become this.

5. Stepped-Rim Coin

A staircase of ascending bars grows straight out of the coin's own edge, as if the coin's rim is generating the chart itself.

6. Portfolio Coin

The coin's face is a struck allocation pie — stocks, funds, gold, cash — literally "your complete financial picture" on a coin.

7. Sprouting Coin

A dominant gold coin with a tiny money-plant poking out of a coin-slot at the top — literal "grow your money," kept small so the coin still leads. (Closest of the ten to the leaf language you said no to — worth judging on its own since it's an accent here, not the whole icon.)

8. Coin Fountain

Coins arcing upward out of a base, mid-air — abundance overflowing, kept elegant rather than a literal coin-rain/jackpot burst.

9. The Nest Egg

The actual English word for savings, drawn literally — one warm gold egg cradled in a nest. Safest, most instantly-readable of the ten.

Top pick
10. Compound Coins

One small coin at the base, two feeding up into one large coin at the top — many small contributions converging and compounding into one outcome. The clearest visual of "chip in, watch it grow" of all ten: coins the whole way through, and the shape itself is the compounding.

Recommendation: #10, Compound Coins. Every element in it is a coin — nothing to decode — and the composition itself (small ones converging upward into one big one) is the growth story, rather than a growth symbol added next to a coin. #9 (Nest Egg) and #2 (Growth Line Coin) are the safest fallbacks if you want zero ambiguity. #7 (Sprouting Coin) is the one most likely to trip the same "plantation" objection — flagged so you can rule it out quickly rather than by surprise.
