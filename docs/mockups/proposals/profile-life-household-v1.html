Notes for review

All options keep the exact same data (relationship, home own/rent, risk appetite, children birth-years) and the "optional / encrypted / 5-year band" reassurance — only the presentation changes.

A — Icon tiles (recommended): reuses the canonical icon-tile selector already used by Employment on this screen, so Edit Profile reads as one consistent system. B — Inline rows: most compact, good if we want the section to take less scroll, but abbreviates risk and shrinks tap targets. C — Tiles + descriptive risk: A plus explained risk cards + a nicer year entry — best for first-timers, a bit taller.

Open questions: (1) icon choices — Married = heart vs rings; Rent = key vs building; Risk = shield/scale/rocket ok? (2) Do you want risk descriptors (C) or keep single-word labels (A)? No app-code changes until you pick.
