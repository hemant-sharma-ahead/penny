"Did You Know" tips — three tiers, one shared content library

A new, previously-nonexistent UI pattern (per docs/DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md's own gap — no coach-mark/tips convention exists today). Three surfaces working together instead of one: contextual nudges earned by real behavior, a passive rotating card in low-stakes spots, and a browsable hub for anyone who wants to see everything at once. Grounded in the one real precedent that already exists — features/tax/DidYouKnow.tsx — generalized rather than duplicated, plus components/ui/Banner.tsx's existing stateless/dismissible contract.

Revised after discussion: Home gets ONE tip a day, right at the very top — highest visibility, since Home is where users spend the most time. Anti-nag rules, explicit: Tiers 1 & 2 draw ONLY from the 39 curated tips — never the fuller catalogue. Only ONE new (never-seen) fact is revealed anywhere per calendar day — whichever surface the user hits first that day "claims" it. Home's daily card runs once through all 39, then stops appearing entirely — by then Discover Penny is the known place to keep exploring, so nothing lingers past its usefulness. A contextual nudge (Tier 1) fires at most once, ever, per fact. Never more than one tip-surface visible on screen at a time. Discover Penny (Tier 3) is the one place that shows everything the research found — curated + the rest — always fully browsable, unaffected by any of the above pacing.

Summary

  • ① Contextual nudges — TipNudgeBanner, a thin wrapper over Banner variant="info", wired at ~5 specific trigger sites (Transactions, Manage Tags, Goals, and a couple more per the plan's trigger list). Fires once per fact, ever. Curated-39 only.
  • ② Rotating card — DidYouKnowCard, generalizing features/tax/DidYouKnow.tsx. One new tip a day at the very top of Home (stops once all 39 are shown), plus its own smaller, context-relevant card at the bottom of Analytics. Tax's own screen becomes a thin wrapper over this same component. Curated-39 only.
  • ③ Discover Penny hub — new Settings sub-page, grouped-by-module + searchable + the Home-card toggle, showing the FULL catalogue (curated 39 + everything else genuinely user-facing the research turned up).

Mobile-only — apps/web-react is frozen, nothing here touches it.
