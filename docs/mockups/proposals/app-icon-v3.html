Penny — App Icon Directions v3

Staying with the leaf/organic-growth identity — that part isn't the problem, so it stays. Two things change this round: the coin should look like an actual coin (dimensional, embossed, a real metal object — reeded edge, curved-metal shading, a specular highlight — not a flat green circle with a cream sticker on it), and the center glyph moves past the static leaf into something that feels alive and inventive rather than a fixed emblem. No cards, currency glyphs, or piggy banks here — all five stay rooted in growth/nature imagery, told in ways nobody else in fintech is using.
Direction A — True Coin (same leaf, now an actual coin)

The exact leaf you already have, unchanged — but the coin body now has real dimension: a radial gradient curving from a lit highlight (top-left) to a deep shadow edge, a dashed reeded rim (a coin's milled edge), a bevel ring, a specular arc, and a soft drop-shadow under the medallion so it looks embossed/stamped rather than pasted flat. The safest, most surgical fix — keeps everything proven, just makes the coin actually look struck from metal.

Direction B — Growth Rings

The coin's face is engraved with concentric tree-growth rings — years of compounding, made literally visible, like a cross-section of a trunk — with a fresh sprout breaking through the rings at the top. It's a coin that reads as both "money" (a struck medallion) and "time turned into growth" at once — nobody else in fintech uses tree-ring imagery. Tradeoff: the individual rings blur into soft banding at 24px (the sprout still reads, the ring count doesn't).

Direction C — Seed Break (germination, in progress)
Most alive / narrative

Instead of a static leaf, the coin's face is a seed pod cracked open down the middle, with a sprout actively pushing up through the gap. It's the same organic language, but caught mid-action — money in the act of becoming growth, not a finished emblem. That's the "alive" quality: it looks like something is happening, which is exactly the kind of icon a user pauses on and wants to open. Reads as a distinctive split-leaf silhouette even at 24px where the sprout thins out.

Minimal deviation from what's proven to scale, with one detail that rewards a closer look. Tradeoff: the zigzag reads clearly at 96px+ (app store, splash) but softens toward a slightly thicker center line at 24px — the leaf silhouette still carries it there.

Direction E — Unfurling Fern Frond (boundless pick)

A fiddlehead fern — a young frond still curled tight, caught in the exact moment before it unfurls. No other fintech app uses this: it's a single bold, confident spiral stroke (with two small bud nubs low on the stem) instead of a static symbol, and it carries real meaning once you know it — potential energy, not-yet-realized growth, coiled and about to open, which is a much more inspiring story for a wealth app than "here's a leaf." One continuous thick gesture with a rounded cap holds up beautifully all the way down to 24px — nothing here to lose at small size. This first pass is a good-enough hand-drawn curl; the exact spiral math is worth a tuning pass once you've picked it.

Recommendation: Direction E (Unfurling Fern Frond). It's the one that actually answers "boundless, modern, alive, unique, appeals to the user to know more" — a coiled frond mid-unfurl is a genuinely new visual metaphor for growth that nobody else owns, it's a single bold gesture that scales perfectly, and "potential energy about to release" is a stronger emotional hook for a wealth app than a finished leaf. If you'd rather change as little as possible while still fixing the "look like a real coin" note, Direction A is the safe, minimal move — same leaf, dimensional coin. Direction C (seed break) is the middle ground: still very novel and "alive," slightly more literal/narrative than the frond.
