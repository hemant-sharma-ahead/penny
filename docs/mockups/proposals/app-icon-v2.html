Penny — App Icon Directions v2

Follow-up to app-icon-v1.html (Direction F, the leaf-medallion coin stack, shipped 2026-08-04 as the real assets/icon.png). Fresh feedback: it doesn't read as money at a glance — the cream teardrop shape reads as a leaf/plant mark before it reads as a coin, so the whole icon lands closer to an eco/wellness app than a finance app. The coin-disc frame (two-tone green rim, deeper offset coin behind for a "stack" feel) was working fine — it's specifically the center glyph that needs to change. All directions below keep that same coin-badge frame and brand palette, but swap the leaf for something that reads as currency with zero interpretation required — most of these lean on the ₹ symbol itself, since it's the one mark that can't be mistaken for anything else and directly signals "India-first money app."

Solid piggy-bank silhouette with a coin slot. Reads as "savings," friendly/approachable tone (fits Chip's personality), but at 24px the legs/ears/tail/snout compress into a single blob and the coin-slot detail (the one piece that says "money" rather than just "pig") is the first thing to vanish — of the four, this one degrades worst at launcher scale.

Recommendation: Direction A (Bold ₹ Coin). It's the smallest change from what's already shipped — same coin-stack frame, same palette, same "solid shapes over line art" lesson already learned from v1 — it just swaps the one glyph that was misreading. It's also the only direction that reads as money at 24px with zero ambiguity, no small details to lose. B (stacked coins + ₹) is the strongest runner-up if you want the "stack" idea more visually prominent, at the cost of more geometry to keep clean across icon densities.
