Two summary-card layouts to pick from — same three numbers (total SIP/mo, total goals, remaining goals), different emphasis:
Option 1 leads with the SIP number as the hero (biggest, most actionable — "this is what you need to be investing monthly, in total, to hit everything"), with goal counts as a small stat row below.
