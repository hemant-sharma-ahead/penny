Home — 3 layout options, dark only

Dropped light-mode side-by-sides per your note — dark only until a direction is picked. All three include the real content you called out (Insurance, Accounts, Tax/Loans/Cash-flow navigation, Financial health, Stories) plus the two future features designed in fully: a retirement-corpus projection toggle and a benchmark line, both built directly into the Net Worth hero chart, not bolted on separately. The "Personal ▾" switcher is now a small floating pill above the tab bar (Home only) instead of sitting inline and taking vertical space.

All three vary both layout and visual treatment together (you asked for both axes at once) — so they're genuinely different bets, not the same grid with different colours. Each is a complete Home, scrollable past what's shown (noted where it continues) — none of these are meant to fit one single screen-height without scrolling, that was never realistic given how much real content Home carries.

Option B

Bento grid — density through grid cells, not stacking

Directly from current fintech-redesign practice (this is the pattern the real HDFC Bank redesign case study used to fix exactly this "too much scrolling" complaint). Everything below the hero sits in a 2-column grid of mixed-size cells instead of one full-width card per section — roughly half the scroll length of A for the same content.

My pick: B, bento grid. It's the one backed by the actual HDFC Bank redesign case study I researched (their whole fix for "too much scrolling, hard to find things" was moving to a bento grid), it fits your "don't waste space" note most literally, and unlike C it doesn't hide real numbers (Insurance, Loans, Accounts) behind an extra tap — those are things you said you do want visible, just not taking a full card each.
