Notes

  • The bottom floating card you flagged is a real inconsistency, not just a look — docs/DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md has a non-negotiable rule ("centered modals, never bottom sheets") that components/ui/Modal.tsx is built around specifically to enforce. Every other tap-for-detail surface in this app (source/tone/holding filters, group switcher, etc.) already uses it.
  • Option 1 (centered Modal) costs almost nothing to build — it's a direct reuse of the existing component with a title + the same mood content already in NewsMoodGauge.tsx, just relocated from an always-visible banner into an on-demand modal body.
  • Option 2 (inline expand) avoids any overlay/dimming at all, which is nice for a quick glance, but it's a new interaction pattern (expand/collapse in place) that doesn't exist anywhere else in the app yet.
  • Option 3 (popover) is the most visually slick but introduces a third overlay style (dim-modal, inline-expand, anchored-popover) for the app to carry going forward — more surface area for one screen's sake.
Recommendation: Option 1. It directly satisfies "no bottom sheets," reuses a component the rest of the app already relies on, and needs the least new code — the mood icon in the tab row just becomes a Pressable that opens the existing Modal with the gauge's content inside.
