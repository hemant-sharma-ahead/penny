Safe to spend — excluding money saved toward goals

Two small additions: a new toggle on the Goal form (default ON — every goal counts unless you turn it off yourself), and the Safe-to-spend hero (Cash Flow page) now shows what it's excluding so the lower number isn't a mystery.

Where each number comes from: Safe to spend = liquid balance (Cash + Bank + Wallet) − upcoming committed outflows − your buffer − money saved across every "counts" goal (Emergency Fund, Home Down Payment, PPF, Retirement... whatever you've flagged on). The daily balance chart further down Cash Flow is untouched — it still shows your real bank balance, since that money hasn't physically gone anywhere. Only the "safe to spend today" figure treats it as already spoken for.
