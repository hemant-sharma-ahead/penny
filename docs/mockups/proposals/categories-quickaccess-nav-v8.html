Categories — Frequent row + sticky group nav

Different angle: instead of compressing the grid further, change how you get to a category. Most taps repeat the same handful of categories — surface those first, zero scroll. For everything else, a sticky chip bar jumps straight to the group instead of a manual scroll. Still your preferred 6-per-row module tile underneath. This one is a live prototype — scroll the body and tap the chips.

Frequent row — top categories by actual usage (txnCountByCategory, already available in CategoryPickerModal — no new data needed). One tap, no scrolling, covers the common case entirely.
Sticky group chips — horizontal, scroll-spied to highlight the group currently in view; tap a chip to jump straight to it. Every group renders the same way regardless of size — no ≤12/>12 special case at all.
