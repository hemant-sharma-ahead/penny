Notes

  • Same underlying data, different disclosure timing. None of these drop the mood gauge, disclaimer, or filters — the SEBI/PII/compliance-relevant disclaimer text stays reachable in all three, just not force-fed above every scroll.
  • Option A (compact strip) is the safest structural change: same always-visible mood signal (now a chip, tap for the full breakdown+disclaimer in a sheet), filters collapse to a single "Filters" pill (opens the existing 2-3-field modal — FilterDropdown already opens a `Modal`, so a combined one is the same pattern, just triggered from one pill instead of 2-3 boxes). Straightforward to build on the existing components.
  • Option B (card-first) is the most aggressive — best card density, but hides the mood reading behind a tap by default, which slightly undercuts "Chip's sentiment read" as a headline feature of this tab.
  • Option C (collapse-on-scroll) preserves the current full layout exactly at rest, so it's the least visually different, but needs real scroll-position-driven animation (Reanimated `useAnimatedScrollHandler` + interpolated height) — more implementation cost for a smaller structural win than A.
  • All three remove the standalone "ET Markets · Mint · RBI · SEBI" line from fixed chrome — attribution is still shown, just inside the mood detail (A/B) or unchanged (C, since C keeps everything at rest).
Recommendation: Option A. It keeps the sentiment read glanceable (unlike B), costs far less to build than the scroll-driven animation in C, and turns 5 stacked fixed rows into 2 — enough to get 4+ cards on screen like your screenshot implies you want, without losing any information, just changing what's default-visible vs. one-tap-away.
