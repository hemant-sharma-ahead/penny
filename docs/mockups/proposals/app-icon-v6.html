Penny — App Icon Directions v6

Starting over from just the two lines that actually matter: "Chip in. Watch it grow." and "Know your complete financial picture — privately." "Chip in" means contribute — put money in — not the AI advisor's name, not a circuit. Green isn't a requirement here; each direction below uses whatever background actually serves its idea. Gold stays, because that lesson was right — it's the fastest way to say "money" without a symbol. What's new is what happens around the coin: each direction is a different, literal answer to "what does it feel like to contribute and watch it grow," built from something nobody's used in a finance app icon before.
Direction A — Golden Dawn (a coin, rising like a sunrise)
Top pick

A gold coin cresting a horizon like a rising sun, rays fanning upward and off the edge of the frame. A coin already looks like a small sun — round, gold, radiant — nobody's leaned into that. "Chip in" is the literal coin; "watch it grow" is a sunrise, the most universal image of something new beginning and expanding there is. Sky gradient replaces the flat green field entirely — this is the boldest departure from anything shown so far, and reads unmistakably as money (still a gold coin) and growth (dawn, light spreading) in the same glance.

On a home screen

Warmest, most distinctive icon in any row — nothing else looks like a sunrise.

Direction B — The Bloom Burst (one coin, becoming many)

One gold coin at the center, six small gem-like sparkles scattered around it at different distances and sizes — one contribution, multiplying outward into many. Deliberately jewel-like rather than a coin-rain/jackpot cliché (which would undercut a serious wealth app), and it directly illustrates compounding: value appearing where there wasn't any before. Risk: at 24px the smaller, farther sparkles shrink to soft dots — the effect reads best at 48px+ and in the app-store listing.

Direction C — The Private Constellation (the "complete picture, privately" half of the promise)

A gold coin at the center of its own small private universe — a few points of light (your accounts, investments, goals) orbiting at different distances on broken, half-drawn rings, against a night sky rather than a flat field. This is the one direction built from the other half of the promise — "complete financial picture, privately" — everything you have, mapped around one center, visible only to you. Most conceptual of the three; least immediately "money-coded" at a glance since the coin itself is smaller relative to the frame.

Recommendation: Direction A (Golden Dawn). It's the one where "obviously a coin" and "obviously growth" are the same shape at the same time — nothing to decode, nothing borrowed from another app category, and it's the most emotionally warm of anything shown across every round so far. Direction B is the strongest alternate if you want the "multiplying" idea more literal; Direction C is worth keeping in mind for a splash screen or "Complete picture" empty-state illustration even if it's not the launcher icon, since it's carrying a different, real half of the promise the other directions don't touch.
