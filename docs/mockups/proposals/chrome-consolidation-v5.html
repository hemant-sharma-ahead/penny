What this replaces, and what's still open

Deletes entirely: MainTabs.tsx's current header row (hamburger/logo/eye/bell) and the separately-mounted ContextSwitcher bar — both gone, no replacement row of any kind at the top. Adds: one small floating cluster (avatar/mode-dot/bell), rendered once, positioned fixed relative to the tab bar so it's identical on every tab and never scrolls away. Home gains a "Personal ▾" pill as its own first content line (not chrome). Portfolio/Goals/Expenses/Chip are untouched beyond losing the redundant global row that used to sit above their own existing headers.

Recommendation: this direction. It's the first pass that doesn't just restyle "a bar at the top" — it questions whether anything needs to be at the top at all once Settings is one avatar tap and Personal/Groups is Home's own content, and answers no. The floating cluster is the one piece of real new surface area, deliberately placed where a thumb already is instead of copying every other app's top-right convention.
Still worth your call:
  • Cluster position: bottom-right (shown) vs. bottom-left vs. bottom-center — bottom-right assumes right-handed one-thumb use, which is the common default but not universal.
  • Whether the amber ring around the avatar (ambient mode signal) is wanted, or redundant now that the whole screen's background already carries the same tint — could drop it and keep the avatar plain.
  • Does the cluster need to be reachable from inside a modal/nested screen too, or is tab-root-only fine (nested screens already have their own back button + content, arguably don't need it)?
