My honest take on your suggestion vs. what I first proposed

I'd originally proposed deep-linking to the Transactions tab with a preset filter (mirroring how "Owed to others" already jumps to the IOU tab). Your in-place-modal idea is better, and I'd go with it — for a concrete reason, not just preference: navigating away loses your scroll position and whatever month/view you had open in Analytics, and you'd have to re-navigate back. A modal keeps you exactly where you were — dismiss it and you're back to browsing, mid-scroll, no state lost. It also sidesteps a real gap I flagged last time: the Filter modal has no tag filter and no "linked to IOU" filter today, so deep-linking those specifically would've meant adding new `FilterState` dimensions just for this. Your approach needs none of that — the caller (Accounts or Analytics, which already has the full `expenses` array in scope) just filters transactions with plain JS (expenses.filter(e => e.accountId === account.id), or by `categoryId`, hashtag, or whatever the synthetic group already tracks) and hands the modal a list. No new filter plumbing at all.

One real technical thing worth flagging: "all transactions for HDFC Savings" over a few years could be hundreds or thousands of rows — not naturally bounded the way, say, Goal's "unlinked transactions" candidate list is. A plain .map() (like `LinkTransactionModal.tsx`'s capped-at-50 picker) would either need a similar hard cap (losing older history silently) or get slow. My plan: reuse the real `TransactionsTab.tsx` renderer itself (already virtualized via `FlashList`, already has the rail-icon/inline-date treatment we just built) inside this new modal, rather than writing a second, simpler list component — same performance characteristics as the main list, and any future improvement to row rendering benefits both places at once.

What I'd build

  • One new shared component, EntityTransactionsModal.tsx (or similar) — { title, subtitle?, statLabel?, statValue?, expenses, categoryMap, accountMap, hashtags, shouldMask, onEdit, onClose } — a centred `Modal` wrapping the real `TransactionsTab`. Every caller (Accounts, Analytics) just computes its own filtered `expenses` array and a title; the modal doesn't know or care whether that's "one account," "one category," "one tag," or "one synthetic Set-Aside group."
  • Accounts: row becomes tappable (small chevron added), opens the modal filtered by `accountId` (or `accountId`/`toAccountId` for transfers), with the account's live balance as the header stat.
  • Analytics: category rows (in the existing expanded breakdown), the intent-group header itself, every Set-Aside line (Lending & IOU, Goal contributions, Family share, per-tag lines), all open the same modal — each already has (or can trivially compute) its own matching transaction ids from data Analytics already holds (`iouLinkedTxnIds`, `goalLinkedTxnIds`, `familyGroupIds`, per-tag hashtag match).
