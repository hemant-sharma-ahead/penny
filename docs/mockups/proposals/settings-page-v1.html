What changed & why

  • Drawer → full page — escapes the cramped 288px width; standard, scalable pattern.
  • Tabs (General · Display & Privacy · Security & Data) — no more one long scroll; each screen has one job.
  • Consistent list rows everywhere — icon + label + control. Replaces the mix of chip-grid + segmented + rows.
  • Modules as a toggle list — legible labels & states, vs. the 9-chip grid with 9px text.
  • Danger zone — Erase-after-fails + Clear-sample isolated in a red group, away from everyday prefs.
  • Ethos — grouped cards, generous spacing, clear hierarchy = uncluttered & modern.
