Penny — App Icon Directions

Today the real apps/mobile launcher icon (assets/icon.png) is a leftover generic blue chevron placeholder — the coin/leaf mark only ever existed as apps/web-react/public/icons/icon.svg and was never wired in. All directions below keep the brand green (--color-primary: #00a86b) and the coin + leaf idea, but push toward reading clearly as a coin at a glance — and, since Groww's own mark is also a thin-stroke sprout, steer away from that so Penny doesn't read as a lookalike at small sizes.

Coin disc with a ₹ glyph — the top stroke tapers into a small leaf instead of a square terminal. Directly ties "coin" (currency, India-first) + "leaf" (growth) into one ownable mark, no other fintech app owns a ₹-leaf ligature. A solid coin disc (two-tone green rim + deeper-green face, like a struck medal) with a solid cream leaf stamped into the center — engraved veins, not thin outlined strokes. Solid silhouettes survive down-scaling far better than line art (this is why most launcher icons — WhatsApp, Google Pay, PhonePe — are solid shapes, not outlines), and a filled leaf-on-a-disc reads as "coin" first, unlike a sprout growing off a rim. Furthest in silhouette from Groww's thin sprout mark.

Same solid leaf-medallion face as C, but the coin's own rim grows four small squared-off pin notches (top/bottom) — the silhouette of an IC chip package wrapped around a coin. Reads as "Penny" (coin, leaf = growth) at a glance, with "Chip" (the AI advisor) as a found-later detail once you know to look for it. Honest tradeoff: at 24px those pin notches are the first thing to disappear (down to a slightly bumpy circle), so the Chip nod only really survives at 48px and up — the coin + leaf reading (Direction C) is what actually carries the icon at real launcher scale.

Two solid overlapping discs instead of D's edge-on ellipse stack — a second coin (deeper green) sits offset behind the front one, showing only a crescent sliver at the bottom-right. The front coin carries C's full leaf medallion face unchanged. Because both coins are solid filled circles (not thin stacked lines), the "more than one coin / wealth accumulating" story from D survives scaling: at 24px the sliver reads as a subtle thicker shadow/bevel on one edge rather than vanishing into mush the way D's stack lines did — still unambiguously a coin with a leaf, with the stack as a bonus detail once you're looking at it larger (96px+, app-store listing, splash screen).

On a home screen

The stacked-coin sliver still reads cleanly at real launcher size next to neighbouring icons.

Recommendation: Direction F. It keeps everything Direction C got right — solid filled shapes that hold up at 24–48px, a coin-first silhouette, distinct from Groww's thin sprout mark — while adding back D's "growing wealth stack" idea via a second overlapping disc rather than thin edge-on ellipse lines, so nothing gets lost at small size the way D did. If you want the stack more prominent (visible even at 24px), the tradeoff is offsetting the coins further apart, but that starts pushing the leaf medallion off-center — happy to tune the offset once you've picked this direction.
