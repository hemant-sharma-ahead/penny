Insurance — redesigned Add/Edit form + Tracked Insurance card (v1)

Grounded in the real current code: apps/mobile/src/features/insurance/{InsurancePage,PolicyForm,PolicyCard,CoverageSummary,useInsurance}.tsx, packages/core/src/core/insurance/meta.ts, and the InsurancePolicy type in packages/core/src/core/db/types/index.ts. Today's model is flat and type-agnostic (type, insurer, policyNumber, coverageAmount, annualPremium, renewalDate, nominees, notes) — no start date, no duration, no payment frequency, no first-year discount, and no type-specific fields at all. This mockup redesigns the Add/Edit form to be type-conditional and the Tracked Insurance card to surface each type's most relevant numbers. The Home-screen "Track insurance" prompt not clearing once a policy exists is a separate, already- flagged code bug — out of scope here. Mockup only — no app code touched.

What's happening when you tap a tile
  • Universal block (name → first-year discount) never moves — same fields, same order, for every type.
  • Duration presets swap per type: Term/Life get year + "till age" chips; Health/Vehicle/Home get shorter renewal-cycle chips; Travel gets day-length chips for a single trip.
  • Picking a chip auto-fills End date from Start date + duration. Tapping End date directly flips the label to "(custom)" and un-selects the chips — either direction works, matching how DateInput already behaves elsewhere.
  • Insurer list re-scopes to the selected type (see caption under the field) — see §③ for the full picker + "Other" states.
  • Premium payment term only appears for Term/Life, and only when frequency ≠ Single — a lump-sum policy has no "years to pay" concept.
  • First-year discount is a real toggle: off by default, and its preview line does the year-1-vs-renewal math live so the interaction is unambiguous.
  • The type-specific block right after the discount is the only part of the form each type fully owns — try Health (family members + co-pay) vs Vehicle (reg. no. + IDV + NCB) vs Term (sum assured + nominee).

Why these hero stats per type
  • Term — sum assured (the number that matters for adequacy), premium, and nominee, plus a progress bar for "years of premium paid" when Limited Pay is set (falls back to just showing "Cover till {year}" when Regular pay/Single premium, so it never renders a meaningless 0-length bar).
  • Health — sum insured, small avatar-initial stack for members covered (glanceable "who's on this" without reading names), and co-pay so an out-of-pocket clause isn't buried in Notes.
  • Vehicle — IDV and NCB together (the two numbers that actually change your payout/renewal quote), registration number moved into the footer since it's an identifier, not a comparison stat.
  • Life/Home/Travel/Other reuse the identical shell at a more compact density — proves the pattern generalizes without needing 7 bespoke layouts.
  • Renewal DueDateBadge and the amber "renews soon" Banner are untouched — same component, same thresholds as today.
⑤ For you to weigh in on

Design decisions made, and open questions

Decisions made (flagging, not asking permission for)

  • Nominee(s) moved from a universal field to Term/Life-only. Today it's a generic optional field on every type; in practice only death-benefit products (Term, Life/Endowment/ULIP) have a nominee. Health uses "members covered" instead, and Vehicle/Home/Travel/Other don't have one at all.
  • "Premium payment term" is a new concept, distinct from policy duration. A Term plan can run 20 years but only require 5 or 10 years of paying (Limited Pay) — this directly answers the "annual payment for 5 years… or lump sum" scenario from the brief. It only shows for Term/Life, and disappears entirely when frequency = Single premium (there's no "years to pay" once it's a one-time payment).
  • First-year discount is modeled as a toggle, not a permanent premium override. The stored annualPremium stays the ongoing/renewal figure; the discount is a one-time year-1 modifier (% or flat ₹) shown as a live "Year 1: ₹X · Year 2 onward: ₹Y" preview, so the two numbers never get confused in the data model.
  • Duration presets are type-specific (Term/Life get multi-year + "till age" chips; Health/Vehicle/Home get shorter renewal-cycle chips; Travel gets day-length chips) rather than one generic set, because a 15-year chip is meaningless for a 2-week travel policy.
  • Health "members covered" is a multi-select chip picker, not a comma-separated free-text field — gives a live count and reuses the same chip-selection interaction pattern already used for duration/NCB elsewhere in this same form, rather than inventing a third input style.
  • Vehicle/Home/Travel share one general-insurer dropdown list rather than three near-identical lists, since these are the same category of insurer (general insurance) in India — reduces list-maintenance burden without losing scoping accuracy (Life/Term and Health still get their own distinct lists).
  • The Tracked Insurance card keeps today's "tap card → open Add/Edit form pre-filled" interaction as its detail view, rather than introducing a separate read-only detail modal — there's no existing detail-popup pattern on this screen to extend, and the richer form now carries enough information density to double as one.

Open questions

  • confirmSwitching the type tile after an insurer is already selected resets the insurer field rather than keeping a now-mismatched selection. Is a silent reset (with the caption updating to show the new scope) the right call, or should it prompt first?
  • confirmThe "Till 60 / Till 65 / Till 70" age-based duration chips (Term/Life) need the user's date of birth to compute a real end date. Penny already stores DOB in Profile — is reading it locally for this non-AI, on-device calculation acceptable, or should these chips be dropped in favor of year-only presets?
  • flagVehicle insurance fields here (registration number, IDV, NCB) overlap in spirit with the existing Real Assets → Vehicle holding's own lightweight insurance-date fields (vehicleInsurancePolicyNo etc. in packages/core/src/core/db/types/index.ts). They're not fully duplicate today — the Vehicle holding only tracks a policy number + compliance dates for its own card, not premium/IDV/NCB — but a user with both a tracked Vehicle asset and a tracked Vehicle insurance policy could end up maintaining the registration number and policy number in two places that can drift. Not resolving this in this mockup (different screens, different original scope) — flagging it now given this project's own documented history with exactly this kind of duplicated-capability drift (Backup & Restore, 2026-07-27), so it's a deliberate "not now" rather than an unnoticed gap.
  • confirmShould a typed "Other" insurer value be remembered and offered as a suggestion the next time that type's dropdown opens (a growing local autocomplete), or always stay a one-off free-text entry with no memory?
  • flagCoverageSummary.tsx (the "Term cover / Health cover / Total annual premium" footer card) is intentionally left unchanged in this round — it still only sums Term/Life/Health coverage amounts. It could be extended once richer per-type data exists (e.g. showing total Vehicle IDV or total Home structure value), but that's a separate, smaller follow-up, not bundled in here.
  • confirmHealth's co-pay is modeled as a single flat %. Some real policies have tiered/room-rent-linked co-pay clauses that don't reduce to one number — is a free-text notes field (already present) an acceptable place for that nuance, or does it need its own structured field?
