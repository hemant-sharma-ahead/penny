Penny — App Icon Directions v4

Clean slate — no leaf, no plant, no currency glyph, no wallet, no piggy bank. The one thing carried forward is the dimensional coin treatment (radial-gradient metal shading, dashed reeded edge, bevel ring, specular highlight) — that read well and has nothing to do with the "looks like plantation" problem, so it stays as the base for all five. The tagline is "Chip in. Watch it grow." and the core promise is "Know your complete financial picture — privately." Every direction below comes from a different piece of that: the act of contributing and watching it compound, the AI advisor Chip itself, upward trajectory, and the privacy promise — told through motion and energy rather than a static object.
Direction A — The Ripple ("Chip in. Watch it grow.", literally)

A single drop lands off-center on the coin's face, and broken, asymmetric ripple rings spread outward from it — fading as they widen. It's the tagline made visual: one small contribution, spreading outward into something bigger. Nothing here has been done by another finance app, it's built from three simple concentric shapes so it survives 24px without losing anything, and it invites a genuine "wait, what is that?" — which is exactly the curiosity you asked for.

An irregular starburst — a bright core with rays at uneven lengths and angles, like a struck match or a synapse firing, not a symmetric sparkle (which reads dated fast). This is Chip itself: your AI advisor as a literal spark of insight sitting inside the coin. Reads as energetic and alive at any size since it's built from straight rays, no fine detail to lose.

A small orb rides a rising trajectory out of a fading comet-tail, breaking toward the coin's upper edge rather than sitting centered and static — motion with no ceiling, exactly "watch it grow" without drawing a bar chart (which every finance app already uses). The gradient trail is the one part that depends on the renderer supporting SVG gradients cleanly at tiny sizes — worth a real-device check at 24px before committing.

Six curved blades arranged like a camera aperture or vault-door mechanism, closing toward a tiny lit center — "we see nothing until you choose to open it," made mechanical and precise instead of illustrative. Distinct from every leaf/coin/wallet trope in fintech, feels engineered and modern, and the pinwheel blade shape reads as motion even standing still. Holds its shape well at small sizes since it's six identical repeated blades, not fine linework.

A vitals monitor's pulse trace running straight across the coin, cropped by the rim on both sides — like a heartbeat reading for your finances, tying directly to the app's own Financial Health score without needing a leaf to say "alive." The most literal "alive" of the five, and the boldest single gesture — reads instantly even at 24px since it's one continuous thick line.

Recommendation: Direction A (The Ripple). It's the only one that's a direct, poetic translation of Penny's own tagline rather than a mood borrowed from somewhere else — one small drop, rings spreading outward, fading as they grow. It's also the simplest geometry of the five, so it's the safest bet at 24px. Direction E (The Ledger Line) is the strongest runner-up if you want something even bolder and more literally "alive" — a single pulse line is about as confident and unmistakable as an icon gesture gets.
