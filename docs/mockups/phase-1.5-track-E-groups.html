Static prototype for direction only — not the real app. Scroll to see each surface; captions say what lives where.

The model, in one paragraph

  • Groups are their own world. A group (Goa Trip, Family) keeps its own shared ledger and balances — fully separate from your personal IOU. The same person can be a private IOU contact and a trip member without the two mixing.
  • You step into a group via a slim context bar under the header. It tints to the group's colour + shows member avatars, so you always know whether you're in Personal or a group.
  • Nav never changes — still Home · Portfolio · Chip · Expenses · Goals. Personal IOU stays a tab inside Expenses. Groups are reached from a Groups card on Home and the context switcher.
  • Adding a shared expense reuses the category picker you already like, then adds who-paid + participants + a split method (Equal by default, power on demand).

Decisions locked (this round)

  • Record-to-account = ON by default, with an account chooser (screens 4, 6, 8). Your bank/cash balance stays live; you can switch which account or turn it off per transaction.
  • Context re-scoping (screens 1→3): the whole app steps into the group; Home becomes the group dashboard.
  • Group-tinted centre FAB while inside a group (screen 3).
  • Groups ≠ personal IOU — never cross-reflected (your correction). A person can be in both, kept separate.
  • Trip/event spending stays out of category analytics (screen 11) — this already exists for events; group-linked trips inherit it.

The two-way flow, summarised

  • From a group (screen 4): add a shared expense → optional real cash-out to your account.
  • From personal (screen 8): a "Share with a group" row on the normal popup → records your cash-out and adds the shared expense to the group in one save.
  • After the fact (screen 9): any transaction's ⋮ → "Share with a group".
  • In a vacation (screens 10–11): link/create a group; expenses split with companions and stay out of daily-living analytics via the trip hashtag.
  • The only bridge to personal money is the opt-in account record (cash-out on spend, cash-in on settle). Balances/"who owes whom" never leak into the personal IOU ledger.
